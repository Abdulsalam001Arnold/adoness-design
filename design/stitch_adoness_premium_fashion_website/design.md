

## Brand
Fashion designer website. Aesthetic: premium, editorial, warm minimal.
Tone: elegant, modern, timeless. Not trendy. Not loud.

## Colours
- Background: #FAF6F1 (warm cream — use for all page backgrounds)
- Foreground: #111111 (near black — headings, body text, primary buttons)
- Muted: #C8C0B8 (soft warm grey — borders, secondary text, dot patterns)
- Accent: #A0866A (warm tan — hover states, subtle highlights)
- Surface: #FFFFFF (pure white — cards, image containers)
- Button background: #111111
- Button text: #FFFFFF

## Typography
- Headings (h1–h3): Large bold serif — Playfair Display or DM Serif Display
- Body / UI: Clean sans-serif — Inter or DM Sans
- Nav links / labels / buttons: uppercase, wide letter spacing (tracking-widest)
- Hero heading: very large, heavy weight, centre aligned

## Spacing & Layout
- Generous whitespace everywhere — fashion sites breathe
- Section padding: large top and bottom (min 80px)
- Cards: rounded corners (rounded-2xl or rounded-3xl)
- Grid layouts: asymmetric, editorial — not uniform boxes

## Components
- Navbar: logo left, nav links centre, icons (search + cart) right
- Buttons: pill shape, dark fill, white text, uppercase label
- Image cards: rounded corners, varied heights, soft shadow
- Dot grid: subtle repeating dot pattern on hero background
- Chatbot: floating bottom-right, dark trigger button, cream modal with dark header

## Do's
- Use generous padding and breathing room
- Keep colour palette strictly to the tokens above
- Serif for anything editorial or heading-level
- Stagger image grids at different heights for visual interest

## Don'ts
- No bright colours, gradients, or neon
- No flat uniform grids where every card is identical height
- No generic blue tech UI for the chatbot
- No tight cramped layouts
